Control your Balboa spa with Homey, either directly over your home network or through the cloud.

Features:
- Spa status and water temperature
- Set the temperature and temperature range
- Toggle heater mode
- Control jets, blowers and lights
- Lock and unlock the panel
- Synchronize the spa clock with Homey
